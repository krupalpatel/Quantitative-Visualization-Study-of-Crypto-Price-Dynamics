# Quantitative-Visualization-Study-of-Crypto-Price-Dynamics
Crypto Market Structure Atlas: Volatility, Cycles &amp; Quantitative Signals

This dataset contains historical Crypto market data Historical.

Fields included:

Date — Trading date
Open — Opening price
High — Highest price during the day
Low — Lowest price during the day
Close — Closing price
Volume — Trading volume

Coverage:
Start – Present

Possible Research Applications:

• Cryptocurrency market structure analysis
• Volatility clustering studies
• Market regime detection
• Quantitative trading strategy development
• Risk management research
• Crypto macro cycle analysis
• Machine learning price forecasting
• Financial time-series modeling

The dataset allows researchers and analysts to explore
long-term Bitcoin market dynamics and behavioral patterns.
